Orthopedic Clinical Services

Evidence-guided evaluations and personalized conservative care plans for bones, joints, spine, and everyday mobility.

Comprehensive Treatments

Bone & Joint Care for Everyday Mobility

Every clinical evaluation is tailored to identify the root cause of discomfort and establish a structured recovery plan.

Knee Pain

Assessment and management of common knee-related concerns, including walking pain, stiffness, clicking sensations, and meniscus or ligament strains.

Joint Pain

Comprehensive care and clinical diagnosis for discomfort, swelling, and movement restriction affecting hips, ankles, elbows, and wrists.

Arthritis Care

Evaluation and personalized conservative management of osteoarthritis and chronic joint inflammation to maintain joint function and reduce discomfort.

Back & Neck Pain

Clinical assessment of common musculoskeletal spinal issues, ergonomic strain, cervical stiffness, and lower back discomfort from daily posture.

Shoulder Discomfort

Specialized evaluation for frozen shoulder, rotator cuff strain, reaching restrictions, and weakness during arm lifting or daily activities.

Sports Injuries

Assessment and recovery planning for sprains, muscle pulls, tendon strains, and athletic trauma to help patients return safely to physical activity.

Fracture & Injury Care

Clinical follow-up, post-cast mobility assessments, healing evaluation, and non-operative care pathways for uncomplicated bone injuries.

Bone Health Guidance

Preventative advice, osteoporosis risk evaluation, calcium and nutrition counseling, and bone density management tailored for older adults.

Symptoms We Evaluate

Are You Experiencing Any of These Concerns?

Everyday symptoms described in simple terms to help you determine when an orthopedic visit is appropriate.

Difficulty walking or climbing stairs
Persistent knee swelling or clicking
Morning joint stiffness lasting >30 mins
Severe lower back pain after sitting
Inability to lift arm overhead
Persistent neck stiffness & headaches
Sudden twist, sprain, or sports trauma
Joint stiffness after fracture healing
