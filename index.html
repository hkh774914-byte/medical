About Our Clinic

Compassionate Orthopedic Care, Close to Home

Committed to helping patients restore their physical ease, mobility, and confidence through meticulous clinical examinations and individualized care plans.

At K.K. Bone & Joint Care, we believe that effective bone and joint treatment begins with attentive listening. Musculoskeletal discomfort, whether resulting from everyday joint wear, sports injury, or age-related arthritis, affects one's daily freedom and well-being.

Our clinic provides residents of Rahon, Nawanshahr, and neighbouring communities with dependable, friendly, and transparent medical consultations without the need to travel long distances for initial evaluations and routine checkups.

Symptoms We Evaluate

Are You Experiencing Any of These Concerns?

Everyday symptoms described in simple patient-friendly terms to help you determine if an orthopedic evaluation is recommended.

Difficulty walking or climbing stairs
Persistent knee swelling or clicking
Morning joint stiffness lasting >30 mins
Severe lower back pain after sitting
Inability to lift arm overhead
Persistent neck stiffness & headaches
Sudden twist, sprain, or sports trauma
Joint stiffness after fracture healing
