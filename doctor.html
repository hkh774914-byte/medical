What to Expect During Your Consultation

A patient-focused consultation designed to give you clarity and actionable guidance.

Unhurried Listening

Every consultation provides sufficient time to discuss where and when pain occurs, daily movement limitations, and previous medical history.

Diagnostic Review

Careful inspection of your existing X-rays or scans, explaining what each finding means in everyday terms without confusing jargon.

Targeted Examination

Physical joint range assessment, alignment testing, and walking checks to understand exactly how the discomfort affects your movement.

Conservative Plan

Focus on non-invasive rehabilitation, targeted strengthening, lifestyle modifications, and follow-up reviews to protect your joints long-term.
