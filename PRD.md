Project Name

K.K. Bone & Joint Care — Premium Medical Clinic Website

Document Type

Website Product Requirements Document

Project Purpose

Create a premium, modern, trustworthy and conversion-focused website for K.K. Bone & Joint Care, a bone and joint care clinic in Rahon, Punjab.

The website will initially function as a high-quality demo/sample website that can be presented to the clinic management. The design should therefore feel production-ready rather than like a generic template.

The primary objective is to establish trust, clearly communicate the clinic's services, make contact/appointment booking easy, and provide patients with essential clinic information.

1. Product Vision

The website should communicate the following impression within the first few seconds:

Professional. Trustworthy. Modern. Caring. Local.

The design should combine the credibility of a medical institution with the warmth of a patient-friendly clinic.

Avoid:

Overly corporate hospital aesthetics
Excessive animations
Generic stock-template appearance
Excessive medical jargon
Unverified medical claims
Fake statistics
Fake patient reviews
Unverified doctor qualifications
Claims such as "100% cure" or "best doctor"

The website should feel like a real clinic's digital presence.

45. Final Design Principle

The website should never feel like it is trying too hard to look "medical."

The desired emotional journey is:

Professional → Trustworthy → Comfortable → Clear → Action

A patient should land on the website and immediately understand:

This is a professional local clinic, I understand what they do, I know where they are, and I know how to contact them.

The website should therefore prioritize trust, clarity, accessibility and conversion over unnecessary visual complexity.
